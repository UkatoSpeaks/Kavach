## How it works

A message, social media post or printed flyer says you can claim cashback, a festival gift or
a lucky-draw prize by scanning a QR code. The QR is actually a UPI payment request to the
scammer, sometimes with the "prize" amount pre-filled. Scanning it and entering your UPI PIN
sends that amount from your account.

## Red flags

- "Scan to get cashback" or "scan to claim your gift".
- The payment screen after scanning shows an amount and a person's name or an odd UPI ID.
- The offer uses the name of a well-known brand or payment app but arrives on WhatsApp or SMS.
- Urgency: "only for the first 100 customers", "offer ends today".

## Example messages

- English: "Diwali bonanza! Scan this QR code to get ₹1,000 cashback instantly in your bank
  account. Offer valid today only."
- Hinglish: "Lucky draw me aapka number select hua hai. Yeh QR scan karo aur ₹2,500 ka gift
  apne account me pao."
- Hindi: "बधाई हो! ₹500 का कैशबैक पाने के लिए यह QR कोड स्कैन करें और अपना पिन डालें।"

## What to do

- Do not scan QR codes that promise money. Scanning a QR code is for paying, not receiving.
- If a payment screen appears after scanning, cancel it.
- Check offers only inside the official app of the brand.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS, call or WhatsApp message on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
