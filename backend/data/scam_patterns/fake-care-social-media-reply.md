## How it works

You post a complaint about a bank, airline, delivery or payment app on social media. Within
minutes a look-alike "support" account, or someone claiming to be from the company, replies
or messages you with a phone number or a form link. They then ask for your account details,
OTP, UPI PIN, or ask you to approve a request or install an app to resolve the complaint.

## Red flags

- A reply from an account that is not the company's verified handle.
- A direct message asking you to call a mobile number or fill a form.
- Requests for card details, OTP or UPI PIN to "verify" you.
- Urgency: "your complaint will be closed if you don't respond in 10 minutes".

## Example messages

- English: "Hi, we saw your complaint about the failed payment. Please WhatsApp our support
  team on this number with your account details so we can process the refund."
- Hinglish: "Aapki complaint humein mil gayi hai. Is form me apna account number aur card
  details bhar dijiye, refund 24 ghante me aa jayega."
- Hindi: "आपकी शिकायत दर्ज हो गई है। रिफंड के लिए इस नंबर पर व्हाट्सऐप करें और अपने खाते की
  जानकारी भेजें।"

## What to do

- Use only the company's verified handle or its official app for complaints.
- Do not share account details, OTP or PIN in messages or forms sent to you.
- Block and report the fake account on the platform.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud calling or WhatsApp number on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
