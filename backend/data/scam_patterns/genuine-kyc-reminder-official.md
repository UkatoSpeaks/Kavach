## How it works

Banks periodically ask customers to update or re-verify KYC documents. A genuine reminder
tells you that KYC is due and asks you to update it through the bank's official app, internet
banking, or by visiting your branch with valid documents. It gives time to act and does not
threaten to block the account today.

## Signs it is genuine

- It directs you to the branch, the official app, or the bank's official website that you
  open yourself.
- No link to click and no mobile number to call.
- No request for OTP, PIN, password or card details.
- Sent from the bank's registered sender ID.

## Example messages

- English: "Dear Customer, periodic updation of your KYC is due. Please visit your home branch
  with valid ID and address proof, or update it in the bank's mobile app."
- Hinglish style: "Your KYC re-verification is due. Kripya apni branch visit karein ya
  official app me update karein."
- Hindi: "प्रिय ग्राहक, आपका KYC अपडेट होना बाकी है। कृपया वैध दस्तावेज़ों के साथ अपनी शाखा में
  जाएँ या बैंक के आधिकारिक ऐप में अपडेट करें।"

## What to do

- Open the bank's app or website yourself, or visit the branch.
- Do not update KYC through links or phone calls.

## When it could be a scam

- A KYC message with a link, a mobile number, or a threat that the account will be blocked
  today is a phishing scam.
