## How it works

Someone posing as a delivery agent, e-commerce support or bank staff says you are owed a
refund, or that your account needs verification. They send a QR code and say scanning it
will credit the refund or complete verification. The QR is a payment request. Entering your
UPI PIN after scanning pays the scammer.

## Red flags

- A refund or verification that needs you to scan a QR code.
- The QR arrives on WhatsApp from a personal number, not through the official app.
- The payment screen shows an amount you are supposed to be "receiving".
- The caller stays on the line and guides you step by step.

## Example messages

- English: "Your order refund of ₹899 is pending. Scan the attached QR code with your UPI app
  to receive the refund in your account."
- Hinglish: "Madam aapka refund atka hua hai. WhatsApp pe QR bheja hai, usko scan karke
  verify kar do, refund turant aa jayega."
- Hindi: "आपके खाते का सत्यापन बाकी है। भेजा गया QR कोड स्कैन करें, रिफंड आपके खाते में आ जाएगा।"

## What to do

- Refunds never need you to scan a QR code or enter a PIN.
- Hang up and check the refund status in the official app or website.
- Delete the QR image and block the number.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud call or WhatsApp number on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
