## How it works

A message claiming to be from the postal service or a courier says your parcel is held at
the warehouse because of an incomplete address, a failed delivery, or unpaid customs duty.
It asks you to update your address or pay a small fee through a link. The page collects card
details and OTPs. A related version asks for large "customs clearance" payments for a gift
parcel from a friend abroad.

## Red flags

- A parcel you were not expecting, or a message with no tracking number you recognise.
- A small fee (₹25, ₹49) to redeliver or release the parcel through a link.
- Short links or domains that are not the courier's official website.
- "Customs duty" requested to a personal account or UPI ID.

## Example messages

- English: "Your package is on hold at our warehouse due to an incomplete address. Update your
  address and pay a ₹29 redelivery charge within 24 hours or it will be returned."
- Hinglish: "Aapka parcel customs me ruka hua hai. Clearance ke liye ₹3,500 duty is UPI pe
  bhejiye, warna parcel wapas chala jayega."
- Hindi: "आपका पार्सल अधूरे पते के कारण रोक दिया गया है। 48 घंटे में लिंक पर जाकर पता अपडेट करें
  और ₹25 शुल्क दें।"

## What to do

- Do not click the link. Track parcels only on the courier's official website or app using a
  tracking number you already have.
- Customs duty is never paid to a person's UPI ID or bank account.
- Delete the message.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS or sender on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
