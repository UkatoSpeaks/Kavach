## How it works

Couriers, the postal service and shopping or food apps send updates about orders you placed:
shipped, out for delivery, delivered, or a failed delivery attempt. They mention your order
or tracking number and the item or restaurant, and point you to the official app or website
for tracking.

## Signs it is genuine

- It matches an order you actually placed.
- It has an order or tracking number you can check in the official app.
- A failed delivery message says they will try again or asks you to reschedule in the app.
- It does not ask for a fee, card details or an address update through an unknown link.

## Example messages

- English: "Your order #40812 with 1 item has been shipped and will be delivered by Thursday.
  Track it in the app."
- Hinglish style: "Your food order from Sharma Dhaba is out for delivery. Ravi aapke paas 15
  min me pahunchega."
- Hindi: "आपका पार्सल आज डिलीवर नहीं हो सका क्योंकि आप उपलब्ध नहीं थे। हम कल फिर से प्रयास
  करेंगे।"

## What to do

- Track the order in the official app or website using your order number.
- Nothing needs to be paid for an order that was already paid for.

## When it could be a scam

- A parcel you never ordered, a small redelivery fee, customs duty, or an address update
  through a link is a phishing scam.
