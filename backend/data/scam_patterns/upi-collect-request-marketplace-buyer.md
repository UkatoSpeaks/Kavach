## How it works

You list something for sale on an online marketplace. A "buyer" agrees to your price without
bargaining, often saying they are in the army or being transferred and cannot come in person.
They say they will pay an advance by UPI, but what arrives is a collect request (payment
request). They tell you to approve it with your UPI PIN so the money reaches you. Approving it
sends money from your account to them. If you question it, they send a bigger request to
"fix" the problem.

## Red flags

- A buyer who agrees instantly, is in a hurry, and will not meet or inspect the item.
- "Accept the request and enter your PIN to receive the advance."
- A second request "to refund the extra amount" after the first one.
- Pressure to move the chat off the marketplace to WhatsApp.

## Example messages

- English: "I will take the fridge at your price. I am sending a request of ₹4,000 as
  advance, just approve it with your UPI PIN and the money will come to you."
- Hinglish: "Bhai main army me hoon, cycle le lunga. Advance ki request bheji hai, approve
  karke PIN daal do, paise aapko mil jayenge."
- Hindi: "मैं आपका सामान खरीदना चाहता हूँ। मैंने पेमेंट रिक्वेस्ट भेजी है, उसे स्वीकार करके पिन डालें
  तो एडवांस आपके खाते में आ जाएगा।"

## What to do

- Never approve a payment request from a buyer. A buyer pays you; you do not approve anything.
- Ask the buyer to pay directly to your UPI ID, and confirm the credit in your bank app before
  handing over the item.
- Block the buyer and report the listing chat to the marketplace.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud call or WhatsApp number on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
