## How it works

After a failed payment, a cancelled order or a complaint, someone posing as the merchant,
bank or payment app says your refund is ready. They send a UPI collect request titled
"Refund", "Reversal" or "Cashback return" and ask you to approve it to get your money back.
Approving the request with your UPI PIN pays the scammer instead.

## Red flags

- A refund that needs you to approve a request or enter your UPI PIN.
- The request comes from a personal-looking UPI ID, not the merchant.
- The caller or message rushes you: "the refund link expires in 10 minutes".
- The refund amount in the request is higher than what you paid.

## Example messages

- English: "Your refund of ₹1,850 for the failed transaction is initiated. Approve the
  refund request in your UPI app with your PIN to receive it."
- Hinglish: "Sir aapka order cancel ho gaya hai, refund ki request bhej di hai. Usko accept
  karke UPI PIN daal dijiye, amount wapas aa jayega."
- Hindi: "आपका रिफंड तैयार है। रिफंड पाने के लिए UPI ऐप में आई रिक्वेस्ट स्वीकार करें और पिन डालें।"

## What to do

- Refunds are credited to you automatically. You never approve a request or enter a PIN
  to get one.
- Check refund status only in the official app or on the merchant's website.
- Decline and block the request.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS, call or WhatsApp message on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
