## How it works

You are told a large amount (a prize, a loan disbursal, an insurance claim, an advance) is
about to be sent to you, but first you must pay a small amount: ₹1 or ₹10 "to verify your UPI
ID", a processing fee, GST, or a "release charge". Once you pay, new charges keep appearing,
or the "verification" payment is really a collect request for a much larger amount.

## Red flags

- You have to pay money in order to receive money.
- "Send ₹1 to verify your account" from someone you do not know.
- Every payment is followed by another fee, tax or penalty.
- The payee is a personal UPI ID, not an organisation.

## Example messages

- English: "Your prize of ₹25,000 is ready. Pay ₹499 processing fee to the UPI ID below to
  release the amount to your account today."
- Hinglish: "Aapka account verify karne ke liye pehle ₹1 bhejo, phir main ₹5,000 advance
  transfer kar dunga."
- Hindi: "आपकी इनाम राशि जारी करने के लिए ₹299 का शुल्क इस UPI आईडी पर भेजें, फिर पूरी राशि
  आपके खाते में आ जाएगी।"

## What to do

- Do not pay anything to receive money. Stop at the first fee.
- To give someone your details for a payment, share only your UPI ID. You never need to
  send money to "verify" it.
- Block the contact.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS, call or WhatsApp message on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
