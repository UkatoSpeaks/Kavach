## How it works

Someone posing as HR from a well-known company or a placement agency offers you a
work-from-home or data-entry job without an interview. To "confirm" the job you must pay a
registration fee, a security deposit, a training kit charge or an ID-card fee. After you pay,
more fees follow, and the job never starts.

## Red flags

- A job offer you did not apply for, with no interview.
- A fee to register, confirm the offer or receive a laptop or ID card.
- The recruiter uses a personal mobile number, a free email address or Telegram.
- Payment asked to a personal UPI ID.

## Example messages

- English: "You are selected for a work-from-home data entry job with a salary of ₹25,000 per
  month. Pay a ₹1,500 registration fee today to confirm your joining."
- Hinglish: "Aapka selection ho gaya hai, ghar se typing ka kaam. Joining ke liye ₹999
  registration fee bhejiye, fir ID card mil jayega."
- Hindi: "आपका चयन वर्क फ्रॉम होम जॉब के लिए हुआ है। नौकरी पक्की करने के लिए ₹2,000 सिक्योरिटी
  डिपॉज़िट जमा करें।"

## What to do

- Do not pay to get a job. Genuine employers do not charge candidates a fee.
- Check the company's official careers page to see if the job exists.
- Block the recruiter.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS, call or WhatsApp message on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
