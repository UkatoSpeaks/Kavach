## How it works

Banks send an SMS or email after every debit or credit on your account or card. The alert
states the amount, the masked account or card number, the date, the other party (merchant or
UPI ID) and a reference number. It is information only. If you do not recognise the
transaction, it tells you to contact the bank on its official number.

## Signs it is genuine

- Sent from the bank's registered sender ID (for example XX-BANKNM), not a 10-digit number.
- Shows only a masked account number such as XX1234.
- Includes a transaction or UPI reference number.
- Does not ask you to click a link, call a mobile number, or send any money.
- The transaction also appears in your bank app or passbook.

## Example messages

- English: "Rs.1,250.00 debited from A/c XX4821 on 14-09-26 to VPA grocery.mart@okhdfc.
  UPI Ref 426178390012. Not you? Call the bank's customer care."
- Hinglish style: "A/c XX7710 credited with Rs.15,000.00 on 01-09-26 by NEFT from ACME PVT
  LTD. Avl Bal Rs.18,420.55."
- Hindi: "आपके खाते XX3302 से 05-09-26 को ₹500.00 डेबिट किए गए। UPI संदर्भ संख्या 426178390012।
  यदि यह लेन-देन आपने नहीं किया है तो बैंक से संपर्क करें।"

## What to do

- No action is needed if you made the transaction.
- If you did not, block UPI or the card from the bank app and call the bank's official number.

## When it could be a scam

- A "credit" SMS from a mobile number followed by a request to return money is a
  sent-by-mistake scam. Check your balance in the bank app.
