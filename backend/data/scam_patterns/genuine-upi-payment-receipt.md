## How it works

After you pay or receive money by UPI, the payment app and the bank confirm it: the amount,
the name and UPI ID of the other person or merchant, the time and a UPI transaction ID.
People also share payment screenshots or receipts with family, shops and friends to show a
payment was made.

## Signs it is genuine

- It matches a payment you made or were expecting.
- The UPI transaction ID can be found in your payment app's history.
- Money received shows in your bank balance, not only in a screenshot.
- Nothing is asked of you afterwards: no PIN, no approval, no refund.

## Example messages

- English: "Payment successful! ₹350 paid to Gupta General Store. UPI transaction ID
  426190118273."
- Hinglish style: "Received ₹1,200 from Anita Verma. Rent ke paise bhej diye, check kar lena."
- Hindi: "₹800 का भुगतान सफल रहा। दुकानदार को पैसे मिल गए हैं, रसीद आपके ऐप में है।"

## What to do

- Keep the transaction ID for your records.
- Confirm money you receive in your bank app before handing over goods.

## When it could be a scam

- A "payment" screenshot with no credit in your bank, or a request to enter your PIN to
  receive money, is a scam.
