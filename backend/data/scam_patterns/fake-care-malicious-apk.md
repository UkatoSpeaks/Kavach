## How it works

Posing as a bank's customer care or rewards team, scammers send a link or a WhatsApp file to
install an app to redeem reward points, get a refund, update KYC or raise a complaint. The
file is an .apk installed from outside the Play Store. Once installed it asks for SMS and
notification permissions, reads your OTPs and forwards them, and may show a fake login page.

## Red flags

- An app sent as a file or link ending in .apk, not from the official app store.
- "Your reward points expire today, install the rewards app to redeem."
- The app asks for permission to read SMS or notifications.
- The message says it is from customer care but comes from a mobile number.

## Example messages

- English: "Customer care: your credit card reward points worth ₹5,850 expire today. Install
  the Rewards app from the link to redeem them now."
- Hinglish: "Aapke reward points aaj expire ho jayenge. Is link se app download karke abhi
  redeem karo."
- Hindi: "आपके रिवॉर्ड पॉइंट आज समाप्त हो जाएँगे। रिडीम करने के लिए भेजा गया ऐप इंस्टॉल करें।"

## What to do

- Install apps only from the official app store, and check the developer name.
- Do not open .apk files received on WhatsApp or SMS.
- If you installed one, uninstall it, remove its permissions, and call your bank.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS or WhatsApp message on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
