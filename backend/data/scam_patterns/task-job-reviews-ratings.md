## How it works

You are offered money for giving ratings or reviews: five-star reviews for hotels or
restaurants on maps, ratings for movies, or reviews for products. After a few paid tasks,
the work moves to a website or app with a "wallet" that shows growing earnings. To withdraw,
or to unlock higher-paying tasks, you are told to deposit money first. The balance is fake
and the deposits are lost.

## Red flags

- Payment per review or rating from an unknown recruiter.
- A website or app with a wallet balance you cannot withdraw.
- "Complete 30 tasks and recharge to unlock withdrawal."
- Recruiters who only chat on WhatsApp or Telegram.

## Example messages

- English: "Earn ₹150 for every 5-star hotel rating on Google Maps. Work from mobile, daily
  income ₹3,000 to ₹6,000. Reply YES to join."
- Hinglish: "Movie ko rating do aur har task pe ₹200 kamao. Ghar se kaam, daily payment,
  WhatsApp pe YES likho."
- Hindi: "होटल को रेटिंग दें और हर रिव्यू पर ₹150 कमाएँ। रोज़ाना भुगतान, मोबाइल से काम करें।"

## What to do

- Do not join. Writing paid fake reviews is not a real job.
- Never deposit money to withdraw "earnings".
- Block the number and leave the group.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS, call or WhatsApp message on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
