## How it works

An SMS, email or WhatsApp message claims your bank account, card, net banking or payment
wallet will be blocked or suspended today because your KYC, PAN or Aadhaar details are not
updated. It includes a link to "update KYC". The link opens a fake bank login page that
collects your customer ID, password, card details and OTPs, which are then used to empty
your account.

## Red flags

- A link in the message to update KYC or PAN. Banks ask you to update KYC through their
  official app, website or branch.
- Threats: "account will be blocked today", "last warning".
- The link's domain is not the bank's official domain (extra words, odd endings like .xyz,
  .top, or a short link).
- Messages from 10-digit mobile numbers claiming to be the bank.

## Example messages

- English: "Dear customer, your bank account will be suspended today as your KYC is pending.
  Update PAN details immediately at the link below to avoid blocking."
- Hinglish: "Aapka bank account aaj band ho jayega kyunki KYC update nahi hai. Turant is link
  pe click karke PAN update karein."
- Hindi: "प्रिय ग्राहक, आपका KYC अपडेट न होने के कारण आपका खाता आज ब्लॉक कर दिया जाएगा। तुरंत
  दिए गए लिंक पर जाकर PAN अपडेट करें।"

## What to do

- Do not click the link or enter any details.
- Check your KYC status in your bank's official app, or visit the branch.
- If you entered details, call your bank at once to block your account access and cards.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS or sender on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
