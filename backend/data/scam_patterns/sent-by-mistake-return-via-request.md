## How it works

After claiming they sent you money by mistake, the scammer makes returning it "easy": they
send a UPI collect request, a payment link or a QR code and ask you to approve it. The amount
on the request is often larger than the claimed mistake, or the request repeats. Approving
with your UPI PIN sends your money to them.

## Red flags

- "I have sent a request, just approve it to return my money."
- The request amount is different from what they claim they sent.
- A link or QR to "reverse" the transaction.
- Your bank app does not show the original credit at all.

## Example messages

- English: "I sent ₹1,500 by mistake. I have raised a payment request for the same amount,
  just accept it and enter your PIN so it comes back to me."
- Hinglish: "Galti se paise aapko gaye hain, maine request bheji hai, bas approve kar do aur
  PIN daal do."
- Hindi: "मैंने गलती से पैसे भेजे थे, वापसी के लिए रिक्वेस्ट भेजी है, बस उसे स्वीकार करके पिन डाल दें।"

## What to do

- First check your bank app to see whether any money actually arrived.
- Never approve collect requests, links or QR codes to "return" money.
- Tell the person to contact their own bank for a reversal, and block them.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS, call or WhatsApp message on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
