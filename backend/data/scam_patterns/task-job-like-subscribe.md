## How it works

A stranger on WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS or social media offers a part-time, work-from-home job
that pays a few hundred to thousands of rupees a day for liking videos, subscribing to
channels or following accounts. The first few tasks really pay small amounts to build trust.
You are then added to a Telegram group and moved on to "prepaid" tasks where you must deposit
money, which you never get back.

## Red flags

- Unsolicited job offers with very high pay for very simple work.
- The "recruiter" claims to be HR from a well-known company but uses a personal number or
  Telegram handle.
- Small payments at first, then requests to deposit money.
- Being moved to Telegram and given a "mentor" or "receptionist".

## Example messages

- English: "Hello, we are hiring for a part-time online job. Just like YouTube videos and earn
  ₹50 per like, up to ₹4,000 daily. Message me on Telegram to start."
- Hinglish: "Part time kaam chahiye? Sirf Instagram pages follow karo aur roz ₹3,000 tak
  kamao. Details ke liye Telegram pe message karo."
- Hindi: "घर बैठे रोज़ ₹2,000 से ₹5,000 कमाएँ, सिर्फ यूट्यूब वीडियो लाइक करके। पार्ट टाइम जॉब के
  लिए टेलीग्राम पर संपर्क करें।"

## What to do

- Ignore and block. Real jobs do not pay for likes or ask you to deposit money.
- Do not share your bank details or UPI ID with the recruiter.
- Leave any Telegram or WhatsApp group you were added to.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS, call or WhatsApp message on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
