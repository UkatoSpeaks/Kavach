## How it works

A fake customer care executive, reached through a fake number or calling you about a
complaint, asks you to install a screen-sharing or remote-access app such as AnyDesk,
TeamViewer QuickSupport or a similar tool "to fix the issue" or "process the refund". Once
you share the access code, they can see your screen, read OTPs and use your banking apps.

## Red flags

- A request to install AnyDesk, QuickSupport, or any screen-sharing app.
- A request to read out a 9-digit or 10-digit access code.
- "Open your UPI app while I guide you."
- The executive stays on the call while you open banking apps.

## Example messages

- English: "To resolve your complaint, please download AnyDesk from the Play Store and share
  the 9-digit code shown on the screen with our technician."
- Hinglish: "Refund ke liye QuickSupport app install karo aur jo code aaye woh mujhe batao,
  main aapka refund khud process kar dunga."
- Hindi: "आपकी शिकायत हल करने के लिए प्ले स्टोर से AnyDesk ऐप डाउनलोड करें और स्क्रीन पर दिख रहा
  कोड हमें बताएँ।"

## What to do

- Never install a screen-sharing app on a caller's instructions, and never share its code.
- If you already did, uninstall it, disconnect from the internet, and call your bank to block
  your accounts and cards.
- Change your banking passwords and UPI PIN from a safe device.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud calling number on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
