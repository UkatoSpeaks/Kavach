## How it works

A message says your electricity connection will be cut tonight because last month's bill
was not updated or paid. It gives a mobile number of an "electricity officer" or a link to
pay. The officer asks you to pay a small amount through a link, or to install a remote-access
or "bill update" app, and then takes money from your account.

## Red flags

- "Your power will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM."
- A personal mobile number for an "electricity officer" instead of the official helpline.
- Payment through a link sent in the message, or a request to install an app.
- The message does not mention your consumer number or bill amount correctly.

## Example messages

- English: "Dear consumer, your electricity will be disconnected tonight as your previous bill
  is not updated. Contact the electricity officer immediately on this number."
- Hinglish: "Aapka bijli ka connection aaj raat kaat diya jayega, pichla bill update nahi hua
  hai. Turant is number pe call karein."
- Hindi: "प्रिय उपभोक्ता, आपका पिछला बिजली बिल अपडेट नहीं हुआ है, आज रात आपकी बिजली काट दी
  जाएगी। तुरंत बिजली अधिकारी से इस नंबर पर संपर्क करें।"

## What to do

- Do not call the number or open the link.
- Check your bill on your electricity provider's official website or app, or on the bill
  itself.
- Never install an app on the instruction of an unknown caller.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS or calling number on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
