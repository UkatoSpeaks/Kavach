## How it works

The scammer says you have won a cashback, reward, scratch card or lucky-draw prize and that
the money is waiting for you. They then send a UPI collect request (a payment request) from
a name that looks official, such as "Rewards Team" or the name of a payment app. The request
screen asks for your UPI PIN. Entering the PIN approves a payment *from* your account to the
scammer. Nothing is ever received.

## Red flags

- You are asked to enter your UPI PIN to "receive" money. The UPI PIN is only needed to send
  money, never to receive it.
- A prize or cashback you never entered for, with a short deadline ("claim before midnight").
- The request comes from an unknown UPI ID, or a display name that imitates a bank or app.
- The amount in the request is the same as the "prize" amount.

## Example messages

- English: "Congrats! A ₹2,500 reward has been credited to your wallet. Accept the request in
  your app and enter your PIN to claim it before midnight."
- Hinglish: "Aapko ₹1,999 ka scratch card reward mila hai. App me aayi request accept karo aur
  PIN daalo, paisa turant account me aa jayega."
- Hindi: "आपको ₹3,000 का कैशबैक मिला है। ऐप में आई रिक्वेस्ट को स्वीकार करें और पैसे पाने के
  लिए अपना UPI पिन डालें।"

## What to do

- Decline the request. Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
- Check rewards only inside the official app. Genuine cashback does not need your PIN.
- Block and report the UPI ID from your payment app.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud SMS, call or WhatsApp message on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
