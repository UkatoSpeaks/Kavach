## How it works

You search online for the customer care number of a bank, airline, food delivery app,
e-commerce site or payment app, often to get a refund. Scammers publish fake numbers on
search results, listing sites and social media. When you call, the "executive" sounds
professional, asks for your card or UPI details, sends a link or collect request "for the
refund", or asks you to install an app.

## Red flags

- A support number that is a regular 10-digit mobile number.
- The executive asks for your UPI PIN, card number, CVV or OTP.
- The refund needs you to pay, approve a request, fill a form through a link or install an app.
- They call back from different numbers and keep you on the line.

## Example messages

- English: "Thank you for calling customer support. To process your refund, fill the form at
  the link we sent and enter the amount and your UPI PIN."
- Hinglish: "Sir aapka refund process karne ke liye jo link bheja hai us pe apna card number
  aur OTP daal dijiye."
- Hindi: "रिफंड के लिए हमारे कस्टमर केयर नंबर पर कॉल करें और बताए गए लिंक पर अपनी जानकारी भरें।"

## What to do

- Get support numbers only from the official app or website, or the back of your card.
- No real support executive asks for your PIN, OTP or CVV.
- Hang up if asked to pay or approve anything for a refund.

## Where to report

- If money was taken, call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and file a
  complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud calling number on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
