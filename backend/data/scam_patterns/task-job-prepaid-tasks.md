## How it works

This is the stage of a task scam where the money is taken. After some small free tasks, you
are told the next task is a "prepaid", "merchant" or "VIP" task: you pay ₹1,000 and get
₹1,300 back. The first one may even pay out. Then the deposits get bigger, and the platform
says your account is frozen, you made an "error", or you must pay a tax or fee to withdraw.
Nothing more is ever paid back.

## Red flags

- You must pay money to do a task or to unlock a higher level.
- A promised fixed return, such as 30% in a few minutes.
- Withdrawals blocked until you pay a "tax", "fee" or "penalty".
- A group of other "members" posting screenshots of big profits.

## Example messages

- English: "Congratulations, you have completed today's free tasks. The next task is a
  prepaid task: deposit ₹2,000 and receive ₹2,600 within 15 minutes."
- Hinglish: "Aapka withdrawal hold pe hai kyunki task me galti hui. Account unfreeze karne ke
  liye ₹5,000 aur deposit karna hoga."
- Hindi: "अगला टास्क प्रीपेड है। ₹1,000 जमा करें और 10 मिनट में ₹1,300 वापस पाएँ।"

## What to do

- Stop paying. Money deposited into these platforms is not returned, and more fees will follow.
- Do not pay a "tax" or "fee" to withdraw.
- Save the chats, transaction IDs and UPI IDs you paid to.

## Where to report

- Call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 as soon as possible and file a complaint at
  cybercrime.gov.in with the transaction details. Also inform your bank.
- Report the fraud WhatsApp or Telegram contact number on Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi).
